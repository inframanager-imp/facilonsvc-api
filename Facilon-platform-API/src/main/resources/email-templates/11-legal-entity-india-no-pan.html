⚠️ CRITICAL REQUIREMENT: PAN is Mandatory for Legal Entities

We noticed that your organization does not have a PAN (Permanent Account Number) registered in India.

As per SEBI, Income Tax Act, and RBI regulations, PAN is mandatory for all legal entities (Indian or foreign) to:

  • ✗ Open corporate investment accounts
  • ✗ Trade in Indian securities
  • ✗ File tax returns and TDS compliance
  • ✗ Complete corporate KYC verification
  • ✗ Remit funds for investments
  • ✗ Claim tax treaty benefits (for foreign entities)

PAN Application for Legal Entities:

Type 1: Indian Registered Companies 🇮🇳

  1. Visit NSDL website: https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam
  2. Select "Application Type": Form 49A (for Indian companies)
  3. Fill company details online
  4. Upload required documents:
    • Certificate of Incorporation
    • Memorandum & Articles of Association
    • Address proof of registered office
    • Board resolution authorizing PAN application
    • Identity proof of authorized signatory
  5. Pay application fee: ₹107 + GST
  6. Receive PAN within: 15-20 days

Type 2: Foreign Companies 🌐

  1. Visit NSDL website (same as above)
  2. Select "Application Type": Form 49AA (for foreign companies)
  3. Fill company details online
  4. Upload required documents:
    • Certificate of Incorporation in home country
    • Notarized and Apostilled incorporation documents
    • Address proof in country of incorporation
    • Tax residency certificate
    • Board resolution/Power of Attorney
    • Identity proof of authorized signatory (passport)
    • Proof of business presence in India (if any)
  5. Pay application fee: Varies by country
  6. Receive PAN within: 20-30 days

⚠️ Important Notes for Foreign Entities:

Document Attestation Requirements:

  • All foreign documents must be notarized
  • Apostille certification required (for Hague Convention countries)
  • Embassy attestation required (for non-Hague countries)
  • Translation to English by certified translator (if original in other language)

Additional Compliance:

  • FEMA compliance documentation
  • Foreign investment approval (if required)
  • Tax treaty certificate (if claiming benefits)
  • Authorized Dealer (bank) relationship in India
