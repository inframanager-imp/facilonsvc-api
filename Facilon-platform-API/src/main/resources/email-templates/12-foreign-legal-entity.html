As a foreign legal entity interested in investing in India, your organization will need to follow the Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) or Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) regulatory framework depending on your investment objectives.

🎯 Foreign Investment Routes in India:

Route 1: Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI) 📊

Best For: Portfolio investments in listed securities

Requirements:

  • ✓ FPI registration with SEBI
  • ✓ Category I, II, or III classification
  • ✓ Indian PAN for the entity
  • ✓ Designated bank account in India
  • ✓ SEBI registration fee: ₹1-5 lakhs (category dependent)

Timeline: 3-6 months
Investment Limits: Subject to sector-specific caps

Route 2: Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) 🏭

Best For: Strategic investments, business operations

Requirements:

  • ✓ Government approval (for sectors requiring it)
  • ✓ Indian subsidiary/branch office (may be required)
  • ✓ FEMA compliance
  • ✓ RBI reporting requirements
  • ✓ Sectoral caps and conditions

Timeline: 2-12 months (depending on sector)
Investment Limits: Varies by sector (0%-100%)

Route 3: Portfolio Management Services (PMS) 💼

Best For: Professionally managed portfolios

Requirements:

  • ✓ Minimum investment: ₹50 lakhs
  • ✓ FPI registration or PAN
  • ✓ KYC compliance
  • ✓ NRI/FPI account with designated bank

Timeline: 1-3 months
Benefits: Professional management, customized strategies
