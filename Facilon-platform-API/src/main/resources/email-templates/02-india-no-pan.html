⚠️ IMPORTANT: PAN is Mandatory for Indian Residents

As per SEBI and Income Tax regulations, PAN is mandatory for all Indian residents to:

  • Open investment accounts
  • Trade in securities
  • File tax returns
  • Complete KYC verification

How to Apply for PAN:

Option 1: Online Application (Fastest) ⚡

  1. Visit NSDL website: https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam
  2. Fill the online application form
  3. Submit documents online
  4. Pay application fee (₹107 for Indian address)
  5. Receive PAN within 15 days

Option 2: Through TIN-FC Centers 🏢

  1. Download Form 49A from Income Tax website
  2. Fill the form
  3. Submit at nearest TIN-Facilitation Center
  4. Pay application fee
  5. Receive PAN within 15-20 days

Option 3: Through Aadhaar e-KYC (Instant) 🚀

  1. Visit: NSDL e-PAN Portal
  2. Provide Aadhaar number
  3. Verify OTP
  4. Instant e-PAN within 10 minutes
  5. No additional documents required

📄 Documents Required for PAN Application:

  • Proof of Identity (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, etc.)
  • Proof of Address (Aadhaar, Utility Bill, Bank Statement, etc.)
  • Date of Birth Proof (Birth Certificate, 10th Certificate, etc.)
  • Recent Photograph

What Happens Next?
