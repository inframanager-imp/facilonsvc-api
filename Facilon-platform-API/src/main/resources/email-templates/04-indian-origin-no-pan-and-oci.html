Why You Need These Documents:

📋 PAN (Permanent Account Number):

  • ✓ Mandatory for all investment transactions in India
  • ✓ Required by SEBI for KYC compliance
  • ✓ Necessary for tax filing and reporting
  • ✓ Can be obtained even if living abroad
  • ✓ Lifetime validity

🛂 OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) Card:

  • ✓ Provides NRI-equivalent investment rights
  • ✓ No RBI approval needed for most investments
  • ✓ Can repatriate investment proceeds
  • ✓ Lifelong multiple entry visa to India
  • ✓ Can purchase property (except agricultural land)
  • ✓ Lifetime validity (with updates at age milestones)
